## 4. Why AT-4b closed

Three formation attempts, every stop at input formation, **no surface or crossover
number read after any failure**: (v1) the scoped value-quantile label shape is
degenerate for max-functionals at beyond-autocorr horizons (damp 0.977+); (v1.1, fixed
pre-read) the matched-functional rolling threshold powered the label at τ=1500 — but no
balanced held-out slice could be formed: the G=300 excursion process is non-stationary
at window scale even under drift-aware calibration (test block: zero positives);
(v1.2, owner-commissioned, the pre-committed final attempt) the AT-2-measured 2M scale
with a leakage-safe blocked alternating split failed earlier still, at horizon formation
(damp ≈ 0.10 at every τ). The stop fired; no v1.3; no rung-1 ledger compute was ever
spent — the two-rung design did exactly its job.
