## The question

The B-v2 positive was CORE-dominated and read below the s_pos gate; A typed the
3.6% dense core a `SLIVER` on *size* alone. Nobody had asked whether that dense
core is itself a **regime-2 witness** — control-sufficient *and* state-insufficient.

## The dense nucleus is a genuine regime-2 witness (both halves), just small

Dense core = samples with ≥ 10 of 50 neighbours within ε_K (A's criterion):
**7,288 samples (3.64%)**.

- **Control-sufficient.** Action-disagree among dense-core near-pairs = **0.0249**
  (directed), vs 0.0333 on the sparse halo and 0.0314 over all near-pairs. The
  nucleus is anchor-like or **tighter** — control-sufficiency is uniform across the
  covered support and *sharpest in the dense core*.
- **State-insufficient.** From A's banked adaptive read on the same dense subset:
  **35,739 of 44,218 dense-core pairs (80.8%) are state-separated** (signature-near
  yet high-mode distance ≥ δ_H = 0.0175), and those 35,739 composed witnesses carry
  action-disagree **0.0295**. Signature-near + high-mode-far + action-agree — the
  regime-2 composition — on 35,739 pairs.

Both halves hold on the same pairs. The only reason no registered gate certifies
it: coverage. The nucleus is 3.6% (below A's 0.10 floor); total near-coverage is
0.469 (below the frozen s_pos 0.50). `paired_fiber_verdict` reads UNDEFINED purely
because the coverage-gated verdict never reached CERTIFIED.

Coverage tracks sample density, which peaks at the attractor's energy mode

## What this clarifies (and does not)

- **Clarifies:** all three apparatus are consistent views of one object — a small
  regime-2 nucleus (both halves present, control-sufficiency actually tightest in
  the core), embedded in a control-sufficient sparse halo. The obstruction to a
  clean forcing-general verdict is **purely geometric** (the matched-Re attractor
  is wider — energy 3.7:1 — so ε_K-fibers cover 3.6% densely / 46.9% at all), **not
  a failure of state-insufficiency or control-sufficiency**, both of which hold
  cleanly wherever resolvable. The regime-2 witness's *structure* generalizes to
  the matched-Re forcing geometry; its *coverage* does not clear the absolute gate.
- **Does not:** certify anything (exploratory, post-hoc, 3.6% nucleus, sub-gate);
  overturn any registered verdict (frozen DEFER / A SLIVER / B fenced-positive all
  stand); reveal scale-invariant structure; promote C1; or make any
  infinite-dimensional claim.
