## Verdict

**H-K3 is now a tiny finite-geometry lemma.** In `F_q^2`, a full line plus up
to `q - 2` points outside that line still casts exactly the same one-direction
shadow. The first possible second-direction break occurs at `q - 1` outside
points.

This upgrades the earlier measured collision receipts:

```text
line plus one point  -> measured scaling law q(q^2 - q + 1)
line plus <= q - 2   -> lemma count q * sum_{i=0}^{q-2} C(q^2 - q, i)
```

The empirical reencoding falsifier remains clear. This is still only a
finite-field projection lemma for the registered workbench shadow; it is not an
extremal Kakeya result and not Euclidean evidence.

## Lemma

Let `L` be a line in `F_q^2`, and let `S` be any subset of `F_q^2 \ L` with
`|S| <= q - 2`. Let:

```text
K = L union S
```

Then the registered direction shadow of `K` covers exactly the direction of
`L`.

Equivalently, for a fixed direction, the one-direction collision class contains

```text
q * sum_{i=0}^{q-2} C(q^2 - q, i)
```

bodies of the form "one line in that direction plus up to `q - 2` outside
points."

Across all directions, the corresponding structured family has

```text
(q + 1) * q * sum_{i=0}^{q-2} C(q^2 - q, i)
```

bodies.

## Proof Sketch

Every line parallel to `L` is disjoint from `L`, so completing a second parallel
line requires `q` outside points.

Every line not parallel to `L` intersects `L` in exactly one point, so completing
a second nonparallel line requires `q - 1` outside points.

Therefore, with at most `q - 2` outside points, `K` cannot contain any full line
except `L`. Its shadow has exactly one covered direction.

This also makes the count unique: in the safe range, each body in the structured
family contains exactly one full line, so it is generated by exactly one choice
of `L` and one outside subset `S`.

The threshold is sharp: choose a nonparallel line `M` through one point of `L`.
Then `M \ L` has exactly `q - 1` points, and

```text
L union (M \ L)
```

covers both the direction of `L` and the direction of `M`.

`scripts/kakeya-workbench-tests.mjs` now includes two additional boundary checks
for every supported `q in {5, 7, 11}`:

1. `T8e line-plus-q-minus-2-safe`: a line plus `q - 2` off-line points still
   has exactly the one-direction shadow.
2. `T8f line-plus-q-minus-1-break`: a line plus the `q - 1` outside points of a
   crossing line covers at least two directions.

Together with `T8d`, this pins the measured receipt, the exact one-point scaling
law, and the generalized lemma boundary.

## Interpretation Boundary

Supports only:

> In the finite-field workbench, the registered direction-coverage bitset is a
> lossy projection, and the one-direction collision class has a closed-form
> finite-geometry family up to the sharp `q - 1` second-direction threshold.

It does not support any claim about Euclidean Kakeya, optimal finite-field
Kakeya sets, maximal-function estimates, or new incidence geometry. The value
is projection discipline: the shadow forgets a large, exactly counted body
family.
