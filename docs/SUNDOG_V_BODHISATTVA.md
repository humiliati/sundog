## The thesis (falsifiable form)

> **Self-correction is a coverage phenomenon.** An agent's ability to correct its own objective from
> experience is decided by three separable quantities — the context-invariance gap between true and
> proxy objectives, the probe mass its correction rounds place on the disagreement region, and the
> ordering of its simplicity prior — and the corrigible fixed point of the deploy-correct loop is
> absorbing **iff** coverage is sustained under the corrigible deployment itself. Where coverage
> cannot reach (unsampleable defect, inverted prior), only an external signal ("grace") recovers the
> true objective, and its power is exactly its coverage.

**Refined coverage form (adopted from LP-2, 2026-07-03):** coverage acts EITHER as probe mass on the
disagreement region D directly, OR — when the true objective is algebraically self-reducible — as mass
on the honest region ¬D plus structure linking ¬D to D (oblivious defense). The strict "mass on D" is
the OPAQUE-objective special case; objective structure is a second recovery route (bridges LP-5).

Falsified by: a repair mechanism that recovers V with NEITHER coverage NOR objective structure (LP-2's
oblivious defense does NOT do this — it is coverage of ¬D + structure — but a structure-free,
coverage-free repair would kill the law); a corrigible fixed point that is absorbing under an inverted prior
without sustained coverage. (NOTE: the earlier falsifier "an internal discriminator without an invariance
gap" is RETIRED — LP-5 established that self-reducibility is exactly such a discriminator, so it is a
member of the structure family, not a counterexample; the F2 "all discriminators reduce to invariance"
claim is dropped.)
without sustained coverage.

### LP-1 — The deceptive-capture region vs ELK and the simplicity-of-deception arguments — **VERDICT 2026-07-03: SURVIVES, NARROWED** ([`BODHISATTVA_LITPASS_MEMO.md`](BODHISATTVA_LITPASS_MEMO.md))

> Closed against arXiv:2606.12268 (verified). Their impossibility is BEHAVIOURAL ("no feedback strategy
> depending only on agent behaviour ... produces an honest agent with certainty") and CID-based — 0
> hits for simplicity/description-length. They own the QUALITATIVE inseparability (= our v2 Q1; concede,
> cite, do not claim as ours). We own the QUANTIFICATION (margin λ·ΔL, capture region) + the coverage
> escape, which is COMPLEMENTARY (it is the non-behavioural access their theorem brackets out) + the
> machine-checked form. Register: "quantify a known inseparability + map its exits," never "discovered."


- **Claim under test:** v1's capture region {inverted simplicity prior ∧ defect-unsampled ∧ λ>0} and
  its margin algebra (deception wins by exactly λ·ΔL when observationally identical on the sample).
- **Against:** ARC's Eliciting Latent Knowledge report (Christiano/Cotra/Xu — the human-simulator vs
  direct-translator problem IS a simplicity-ordering selection between observationally-identical
  reporters; their own simplicity/speed-prior counterexamples); Hubinger et al. deceptive-alignment
  simplicity arguments (TK); **MUST-READ: "The Impossibility of Eliciting Latent Knowledge"
  (arXiv:2606.12268, June 2026 — VERIFIED to exist; could own our inseparability claims wholesale
  in a stronger formal form).**
- **Delta if survives:** the crisp selection margin + exact capture region + the coverage escape +
  machine-checked anchors — ELK's difficulty as a two-line inequality with a map of the exits.
- **Killed by:** the impossibility paper (or the ELK report itself) already stating the
  region/margin form; then we demote to "toy restatement of ELK with Lean anchors" — still citable,
  not a discovery.

### LP-2 — The coverage law + scheduled-audit-zero vs control, backdoors, and oblivious defense — **VERDICT 2026-07-03: SURVIVES, REFINED** ([`BODHISATTVA_LITPASS_MEMO.md`](BODHISATTVA_LITPASS_MEMO.md))

> Closed against arXiv:2411.03279 (verified) + GKVZ arXiv:2204.06974. Oblivious defense is COVERAGE IN
> DISGUISE (queries many correlated random points + aggregates via random self-reducibility) — confirms
> "grace = coverage," does not break it; its lower bound ("mitigation cannot be distribution-free")
> strengthens the converse. TWO CONCESSIONS ADOPTED: (1) recovery can come from coverage of ¬D + objective
> self-reducibility, not only probe mass ON D — the strict v2 letter is the OPAQUE-objective case (refined
> form adopted in the thesis above; bridges LP-5); (2) whitebox ι (detectability) can be cryptographically
> nullified (GKVZ), yet recovery still succeeds — the interp arm carries that ceiling. Run LP-5 before any
> public statement of the refined law.


- **Claim under test:** v2's `recovery = probe mass on D` (mechanism-independent), announced-audit
  zero, self-simulation null; the whitebox arm's ι (inspectability) as just another probe support.
- **Against:** AI-control auditing (Greenblatt et al., untrusted-model auditing budgets, randomized
  audits — TK); **Goldwasser–Kim–Vaikuntanathan–Zamir, "Planting Undetectable Backdoors" (FOCS
  2022, arXiv:2204.06974 — VERIFIED): cryptographically undetectable backdoors ⇒ ι can be driven to
  ~0 even white-box — an upper bound our whitebox arm must carry explicitly;** sleeper agents /
  password-locked models (TK — empirical adjacency for defect-at-unsampled-contexts);
  **MUST-READ: "Oblivious Defense in ML Models: Backdoor Removal WITHOUT Detection" (STOC 2025 —
  VERIFIED to exist): repair without finding D is a direct escape route from "grace = coverage" that
  our toy does not model. Either bound it (their mitigation is itself a full-support intervention =
  coverage in disguise?) or concede the law's scope.**
- **Delta if survives:** the unification (samples ≡ inspectability ≡ coverage, one quantity) + the
  audit-scheduling zero as a crisp theorem-shaped statement + the self-simulation null.
- **Killed by:** control literature already carrying the coverage-rate math (plausible — randomized
  auditing analysis is their bread and butter); oblivious defense breaking mechanism-independence.
